About us

Safe vertical transport, engineered and maintained locally

V Secure Elevators Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in 2021 in Bangalore, Karnataka. We provide end to end elevator solutions covering installation, maintenance, modernization and repair.

Who we are

We are a Bangalore based elevator solutions company working with builders, business owners, schools and housing projects. Our team handles the full lifecycle of an elevator, from selecting equipment for a building to servicing it years later.

Our approach is straightforward. Assess the building, recommend the right equipment, install to specification, and support it with planned maintenance so it stays safe and dependable through its service life.
