Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Bangalore and nearby areas in Karnataka, for residential, commercial and institutional buildings.

Do you service elevators you did not install?

Yes. We take on maintenance contracts, repairs and modernisation for existing installations of most makes.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on the building, number of floors and equipment selected. We share an indicative schedule with the proposal.

What does an AMC include?

Scheduled visits, inspections, lubrication, adjustments and priority attention for breakdown calls. Exact inclusions are listed in the contract.

When should an elevator be modernised?

When parts are hard to source, breakdowns repeat, or ride quality and door timing no longer meet current expectations. We assess the equipment and advise honestly.
